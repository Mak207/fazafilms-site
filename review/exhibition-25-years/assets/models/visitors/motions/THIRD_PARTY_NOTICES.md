## Direct source of the v43 turn clips

The separate `emma-native-turns.glb` and `ethan-native-turns.glb` files are derived from Microsoft's female/male `turn_left_90`, `turn_right_90`, `turn_left_180`, and `turn_right_180` FBX animations under `Assets/Animations/all_animations_max_motextr_xyz/`.

Pinned source: https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9

The Microsoft copyright and complete MIT permission notice above apply to these derived animations. The FBX files are converted and retargeted offline; their original movement is separate from the exhibition's navigation controller.
