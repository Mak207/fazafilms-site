Visitor motion clips
====================

The visitor models use retargeted full-body clips from Mesh2Motion:
https://github.com/Mesh2Motion/mesh2motion-app

Mesh2Motion states that its 3D models, rigs and animations are released under
CC0. The application code is MIT licensed. The source clips were retargeted to
the exhibition visitor rigs, grounded, constrained to a 0.72 m action radius
and compressed for real-time web delivery.

Included motion families: greeting and social gestures, kneeling and
meditation, fitness, jumping, evasive movement, dance, victory gestures,
kicking and acrobatics.
