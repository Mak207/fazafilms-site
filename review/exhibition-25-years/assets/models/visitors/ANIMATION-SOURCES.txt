Visitor motion sources
======================

The production visitor models use the original, verified Walk and Observe
clips embedded in walking-man.glb and walking-woman.glb. Locomotion is owned by
the scene controller; the skeleton never translates the character root.

Short exhibition gestures are procedural, stationary upper-body overlays. They
do not modify the hips, legs, feet or character root. Feet stay grounded and
the navigation controller keeps a visitor capsule outside inflated exhibit and
plinth footprints.

The previously tested Mesh2Motion full-body retargets were removed from the
production build after visual QA exposed incompatible leg-chain retargeting.
They are not shipped or used by the pavilion.
